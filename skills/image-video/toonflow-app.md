# Toonflow App

## Description

An open-source AI video creation platform for short dramas, comic videos (`manju`), and animated storytelling using an infinite canvas workspace and AI agents.

## What it does

Parses raw story text, manages character and scene consistency assets, generates outlines and storyboards, and orchestrates multi-provider AI image and video generation with local deployment support.

## Best for

- Animated storytelling, comic-style videos, and short drama production
- Consistent character and scene asset management across episodes
- Teams wanting an infinite-canvas storyboard workflow

## Verdict

PROMISING — well-starred MIT-licensed visual storyboarding and animation platform; best suited for narrative/comic video rather than general coding workflows.

## Limitations

- Requires configuring external or local image/video generation providers.
- Repository documentation reviewed; not personally deployed.

## Risk

MIT-licensed open-source project; inspect provider API configurations before local deployment.
