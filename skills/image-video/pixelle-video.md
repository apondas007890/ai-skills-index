# Pixelle-Video

## Description

An automated AI short-video engine by `ATH-MaaS` that generates scripts, AI images, voice narration, and background music from a single text topic using `ComfyUI` and multiple LLMs.

## What it does

Connects LLMs (`GPT`, `Qwen`, `DeepSeek`, `Ollama`) with `ComfyUI` image pipelines and TTS/audio synthesis to produce vertical or horizontal short videos from topic prompts.

## Best for

- Automated faceless YouTube Shorts, TikTok, or Reels generation
- Local or hybrid `Ollama` + `ComfyUI` video workflows
- Batch short-video prototyping from topic lists

## Verdict

GOOD — practical end-to-end short-video generator with `ComfyUI` integration and flexible LLM support.

## Limitations

- Requires `ComfyUI` and model weights/API keys for image, voice, and LLM steps.
