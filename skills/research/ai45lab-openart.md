## Description

OpenART is an open-source framework designed to evaluate the safety and robustness of autonomous AI agents in dynamic, long-horizon, and stateful environments. It stress-tests agent runtimes against multi-step state poisoning, privilege escalation, and tool-use vulnerabilities across 10,000+ benchmark scenarios.
